Collaborated with software engineers, behavioral science, and product design teams to build on the intelligence underlying a practice test delivery system. My role was to support machine learning operatins.

When starting at CAI, I was tasked with improving the intelligence underling an educational technology tool called Problem Roulette (PR). PR is an online service that delivers practice test questions. Many similar tools exist on the market, so the main value proposition at the time was that its test bank consisted of real, previously used test questions. 

Brainstorming around what this intelligence would empower revolved around two themes: (1) personalization, and (2) gamification. We wanted the user experience to be efficient. That is, to answer the most relevant questions for them personally and to answer the fewest number of questions that they personally needed in order to maximize their grade gain. In particular, the goal was to personalize question difficulty and study volume according to each students abilities and needs.

1) Implement capacity for personalized messages to nudge study volume via proficiency score
2) Improve user experience via intelligent question selection algorithm

In addition to improving user experience through personalization, we also aimed to improve it through gamification. To that end, we wanted students to earn badges associated with predictors of success. Internal research suggested two key predictor of success were (1) study volume, and (2) study accuracy (specifically, stringing correct answers together in a row, which we call 'streaks').
