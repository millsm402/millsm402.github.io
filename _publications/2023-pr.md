excerpt: 'Problem Roulette (PR) is an online study tool at the University of Michigan that offers points-free practice to students in introductory STEM courses. Using four years of data, we analyzed millions of problem attempts in physics and found that students who practiced more tended to earn significantly higher final grades. Students’ grades rose consistently, with the largest gains occurring as they began answering more questions. These gains held across different math backgrounds and demographic groups, though underrepresented and first-generation students earned slightly lower grades than expected. Our findings suggest increased practice helps most students and may be especially valuable for targeted support.'
