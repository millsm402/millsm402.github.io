excerpt: 'This study (my very first!) used multilevel modeling to examine how different viewing tasks influence eye movements during scene perception. While prior research showed task effects mainly on spatial parameters during object-specific search, this study found that open-ended tasks affected both spatial (e.g., saccade amplitude) and temporal (e.g., fixation duration) characteristics. Task set also influenced how fixation durations changed over time but not saccade amplitude, suggesting independent mechanisms govern spatial and temporal aspects of eye movement control.'
